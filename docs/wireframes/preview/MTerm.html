3
Changes

On the 20th, Tom moves to a new city and wants to add collision coverage. His insurer makes both changes with an endorsement.

4
Result

The new address and added coverage raise his premium to $120/month, starting the 20th.

5
Renewal

At renewal, the policy continues with the new address and coverage included.
